Human Transformation
The framework of transformation, the four values (Pagkamaka-Diyos, Pagkamaka-Tao, Pagkamaka-Bayan, Pagkamaka-Kalikasan), and the virtues

FRAMEWORK OF TRANSFORMATION
⚙ The framework has three parts: INPUT, TRANSFORMATION PROCESS, and OUTPUT.
★ Input: Youth Organizations, Family, Community, and Institutions.
★ Transformation process: A. Values and B. Virtues.
★ Output: National Development.
EASIER: Youth groups, families, communities, and institutions shape a person through values and virtues, and transformed people build the nation.
⚙ The four values: 1. Pagkamaka-Diyos, 2. Pagkamaka-Tao, 3. Pagkamaka-Bayan, 4. Pagkamaka-Kalikasan.
Added: Maka-Diyos means for God, Maka-Tao means for people, Maka-Bayan means for the country, and Maka-Kalikasan means for nature. These are the four core values in the Philippine national motto.
★ Value: a belief or principle a person considers important and lets guide their choices.
★ Virtue: a good habit of acting rightly, built by practicing it again and again.
Difference: values are what you consider important; virtues are the good habits you practice.

PAGKAMAKA-DIYOS (LOVE OF GOD)
⚙ A. F.A.I.T.H.: Fear of the Lord, Action, Integrity, Truth, Hope.
⚙ B. Respect for Life.
⚙ C. Order.
⚙ D. Work.
⚙ E. Concern for the family and future generation.
"Let's put fear in its place: fear God, not man." (Kathy Carlton Willis, quoted in the lesson)
Added: what each part of F.A.I.T.H. means.
★ Fear of the Lord: deep respect and reverence for God that guides how you live.
★ Action: faith shown through deeds, not only words.
★ Integrity: being honest and having strong moral principles, the same in private and in public.
★ Truth: being honest and living by what is true.
★ Hope: trust that good will come, even in hard times.
Added: what the other Pagkamaka-Diyos values mean.
★ Respect for Life: valuing every human life as a gift from God.
★ Order: living with discipline and respect for rules, so things run in peace.
★ Work: doing one's tasks well as a way of serving God and others.
★ Concern for the family and future generation: caring for your family and making choices that protect the people who come after you.

PAGKAMAKA-TAO (LOVE OF PEOPLE)
⚙ Love of self, family, and others.
⚙ Freedom.
⚙ Truth.
⚙ Justice.
Added: what each one means.
★ Love of self, family, and others: caring for your own well-being and for the people around you.
★ Freedom: the ability to choose and act, used responsibly and without harming others.
★ Truth: honesty in what we say and do with others.
★ Justice: giving each person what is due to them; treating people fairly.
⚙ The lesson pairs Pagkamaka-Tao with Maslow's hierarchy of needs.
★ Maslow's hierarchy of needs (from bottom to top): physiological, safety, love and belonging, esteem, self-actualization.
★ Physiological needs: food, water, sleep, shelter.
★ Safety needs: security, health, a stable home and income.
★ Love and belonging: family, friends, and acceptance.
★ Esteem: respect from others and self-respect.
★ Self-actualization: becoming the best you can be; reaching your full potential.
EASIER: Basic needs at the bottom must be met first before a person can reach the top.

PAGKAMAKA-BAYAN (LOVE OF COUNTRY)
⚙ The slide also labels it Makabayan.
⚙ Unity.
⚙ Equality.
⚙ Respect for law and government.
⚙ Patriotism.
⚙ Promotion of the common good.
Added: what each one means.
★ Unity: working together as one people despite differences.
★ Equality: everyone has the same rights and dignity.
★ Respect for law and government: following the law and respecting lawful authority.
★ Patriotism: love for and devotion to one's country.
★ Common good: what benefits the whole community, not just a few.

PAGKAMAKA-KALIKASAN (LOVE OF NATURE)
⚙ Concern for the environment.
Added: caring for nature, such as proper waste disposal, saving water and energy, and planting trees, so the earth stays livable for future generations.

VIRTUES
⚙ The framework slide lists nine virtues: Prudence, Justice, Fortitude, Temperance, Industry, Loyalty, Responsibility, Cheerfulness, Generosity.
The virtues slide adds a tenth, Magnanimity. The framework slide stops at Generosity, so the two slides do not match. Know both.
Added: what each virtue means.
★ Prudence: good judgment; thinking before acting and choosing wisely.
★ Justice: giving others what is due to them.
★ Fortitude: courage and strength to do what is right even when it is hard.
★ Temperance: self-control; moderation in wants and pleasures.
★ Industry: hard work and diligence.
★ Loyalty: being faithful to people, commitments, and one's country.
★ Responsibility: doing your duties and accepting the results of your actions.
★ Cheerfulness: a positive, joyful attitude.
★ Generosity: willingly sharing time, help, and things with others.
★ Magnanimity: greatness of spirit; being big-hearted, forgiving, and aiming for great and noble things.
Added: prudence, justice, fortitude, and temperance are traditionally called the four cardinal virtues.
Difference: fortitude is courage when things are hard; temperance is self-control over wants.
Difference: Justice appears twice in the lesson: as a value under Pagkamaka-Tao and as a virtue.

COMMON MISTAKES
⚙ Putting National Development in the input. It is the output.
⚙ Putting Values and Virtues in the input. They are the transformation process.
⚙ Mixing up the parts of F.A.I.T.H.: Fear of the Lord, Action, Integrity, Truth, Hope.
⚙ Placing Unity or Patriotism under Pagkamaka-Tao. They belong to Pagkamaka-Bayan.
⚙ Placing Freedom under Pagkamaka-Bayan. It belongs to Pagkamaka-Tao.

EXAM TIPS
⚙ Know input, process, and output of the framework.
⚙ Know which items go under each of the four values.
⚙ Know what F.A.I.T.H. stands for.
⚙ Know the virtues, including Magnanimity from the virtues slide.

SELF-TEST
Q: What is the output of the framework of transformation?
★ National Development.
Q: What does the A in F.A.I.T.H. stand for?
★ Action.
Q: Under which value does Patriotism fall?
★ Pagkamaka-Bayan.
Q: Which virtue means self-control or moderation?
★ Temperance.
Q: What is the only item under Pagkamaka-Kalikasan?
★ Concern for the environment.
