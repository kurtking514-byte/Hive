Lecture 5 The Human: Emotions
CC7 Human Computer Interaction: why emotions matter, their effects on design, and the three levels of processing

1. Why emotions matter
★ Emotions make the human smart.
★ Much of human behavior is subconscious.
★ The affective system helps a person make judgments.
★ People without emotions cannot choose between alternatives.
EASIER: Emotion is what tips a decision. Without it, a person cannot pick one option over another.

2. Effects of using emotions in design
★ Using emotions in design broadens thorough processes.
★ Using emotions in design brings greater creativity.
★ Using emotions in design brings greater imagination.
★ Using emotions in design makes people more tolerant of minor difficulties.
EASIER: An emotionally engaged user thinks more widely, creates and imagines more, and forgives small flaws.

3. The three levels of emotion-based processing
★ Emotion-based processing has three levels: visceral, behavioral, and reflective.
EASIER: Visceral is the look, behavioral is the use, reflective is the self-image and memories.

4. Visceral level
★ Visceral processing is pre-conscious and pre-thought.
★ Appearance matters at the visceral level.
★ First impressions are formed at the visceral level.
★ The visceral level covers the initial impact of the product: touch, feel, and appearance.
Slide image: a kitten appears on the visceral slide, with no caption in the source.
EASIER: Visceral is the gut reaction that happens before you think.

5. Behavioral level
★ The behavioral level focuses on the use of the product.
★ Behavioral covers the experience with the product: function, performance, and usability.
Slide images: two chairs appear on the behavioral slide, with no caption in the source.
EASIER: Behavioral asks whether the product works well and is good to use.

6. Reflective level
★ The reflective level is affected by culture, experience, education, and individual differences.
★ The reflective level can override the visceral and behavioral levels.
★ The reflective level involves sophistication versus popularity.
★ The reflective level involves long-term relationships with the product.
★ The reflective level involves consideration of the future.
Slide images: two kinds of wristwatch appear on the reflective slide, with no caption in the source.
EASIER: Reflective is the thinking-it-over level, and it can overrule a gut reaction or a good use experience.

7. Working with the three levels
⚙ Visceral: design for appearance.
⚙ Behavioral: design for pleasure and effectiveness of use.
⚙ Reflective: design for self-image, personal satisfaction, and memories.
EASIER: Look for visceral, use for behavioral, and who I am and what I remember for reflective.
