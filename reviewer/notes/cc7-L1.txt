WHAT IS HCI
★ HCI (Human Computer Interaction) is a multidisciplinary field of study on the design of computer technology and the interaction between humans and computers.
HCI is concerned with the physical, psychological, and theoretical aspects of these processes.
HCI has since expanded to cover almost all forms of information technology design.
★ Second definition: HCI is the field of study that focuses on optimizing how users and computers interact by designing interactive computer interfaces that satisfy users' needs.
★ Misconception: HCI only involves physical interactions with a computer.
Fact: HCI encompasses all interactions between humans and digital devices.
EASIER: HCI is not just typing and clicking, it covers every way people deal with digital devices.

HISTORY OF HCI
HCI emerged in the 1980s, when personal computing was on the rise and desktop computers started appearing in households and corporate offices.
HCI's journey began with video games, word processors, and numerical units.
With the advent of the internet and the explosion of mobile and diversified technologies such as voice-based and Internet of Things (IoT), computing became omnipresent and omnipotent.
Technological competence further led to the evolution of user interactions.
Consequently, the need for a tool that would make man-machine interactions more human-like grew significantly.
This established HCI as a technology, bringing cognitive engineering, linguistics, neuroscience, and other fields under its realm.
Today HCI focuses on designing, implementing, and evaluating interactive interfaces that enhance user experience using computing devices.
Today HCI includes user interface design, user-centered design, and user experience design.
EASIER: Early desktops and games, then internet, mobile, voice and IoT everywhere, then a push for human-like interaction, now designing and evaluating interfaces for good user experience.

WHY HCI
Errors and mistakes in computer technologies still exist today.
Error cause: focus on whether or not the technology works.
Error cause: the concept is too cool or groundbreaking.
Error cause: functions are hard to utilize or process.
Error cause: specific people use things specifically.
Error cause: too much safety or security in the technology.
Error cause: too much focus on the design.
⚙ When creating technologies, it must be: suitable for the task; easy to use; adaptable to the user's knowledge and experience; provides feedback on the performance; displays information in a format and pace understandable to the user; conforms to the principles of software ergonomics.
The idea of use is also necessary for HCI.
★ Useful means accomplish what is required, for example play music, cook dinner, or format a document.
★ Usable means do it easily and naturally, without danger of error.
★ Used means make people want to use it: be attractive, engaging, fun.
EASIER: Useful does the job, Usable is easy and safe, Used is something people actually want to use.

FOUR COMPONENTS OF HCI
★ The four components of HCI are Human, Computer, Interaction, and Context.
⚙ Each component answers a question: Human is who the HCI is for, Computer is where the HCI is found, Interaction is what the HCI is, Context is how the HCI will be formed.
EASIER: Human = who, Computer = where, Interaction = what, Context = how.

THE HUMAN
★ The Human is also known as the user or end-user.
Humans are limited in their capacity to process information, which has important implications for design.
The Human component focuses on who the HCI is for.
Information is received and responses given via several input and output channels: Visual, Auditory, Haptic channel, and Movement.
★ Information is stored in memory of three types: Sensory memory, Short-term (working) memory, and Long-term memory.
Information is processed and applied through reasoning, problem solving, skill acquisition, and error.
Emotion influences human capabilities.
Users share common capabilities but are individuals with differences.

THE COMPUTER
★ The Computer component focuses on where the HCI is found.
The Computer comprises various elements, each of which affects the user of the system.
Computer elements: input devices, output display, virtual reality systems, various displays (physical controls, haptic dieback [sic], sensors), paper output and input, memory, and processing.

THE INTERACTION
★ Interaction models help us understand what is going on in the interaction between user and system.
Interaction models address the translations between what the user wants and what the system does.
The Interaction component focuses on what the HCI is.
★ Ergonomics are the physical characteristics of the interaction and how these influence its effectiveness.
Dialog between user and system is influenced by the style of the interface.
Interaction takes place within a social and organizational context that affects both user and system.

THE CONTEXT
★ Context is also known as paradigms.
Paradigms are examples of effective strategies for building interactive systems, used to design usable interactive systems.
The Context component focuses on how the HCI will be formed.
Context ranges from the introduction of timesharing computers, through the WIMP and web, to ubiquitous and context-aware computing.
EASIER: Paradigms move from timesharing, to WIMP and web, to ubiquitous and context-aware computing.
