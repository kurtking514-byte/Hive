Lesson 3 UC-NSTP and Volunteerism
Filipino culture and the common good, bayanihan, the three elements of volunteerism, why people volunteer, modalities, benefits, and volunteering in the Philippines today

FILIPINO CULTURE AND THE COMMON GOOD
★ Filipino culture: a combination of indigenous practices and foreign influences that molded the Filipino character through the years.
⚙ The preamble of the 1987 Philippine Constitution states that the role of the government is to promote the common good.
⚙ The Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2017-2022 espouses values for the common good, cultivating creativity, and strengthening culture-sensitive governance and development.
⚙ Promoting Philippine culture and values is the first step to regain the Filipino spirit of nationalism and pride and to enhance the social fabric, or "malasakit", in every Filipino.
⚙ One PDP strategy is to mainstream cultural education in basic, technical, vocational, and higher education. So the academe (schools) plays a pivotal role.
The reading cites the PDP 2017-2022, which has since been replaced by the PDP 2023-2028. Answer with 2017-2022 if the exam asks about the reading.
★ Malasakit: genuine concern and care for others.
★ Common good (public good): the just and fair distribution of material goods, freedom, and rights in society. It means the social systems, institutions, and environments we all depend on work in a way that benefits all people.
⚙ To achieve the common good, the government, the private sector, the community, and the citizens must work together.

BAYANIHAN
★ Bayanihan: a value for the common good that translates volunteerism and has become a symbol of the Filipino national character.
⚙ It comes from "bayani" (hero) and can also come from "bayan" (town or community).
⚙ So bayanihan means a heroic act, or people working together for mutual benefit.
EASIER: Bayanihan is the classic picture of neighbors carrying a whole house together to move it, for free.

THREE BASIC ELEMENTS OF VOLUNTEERISM
★ Free will: volunteering is the choice of a person or group, without pressure or force from others.
★ No material reward: volunteers do not expect financial or material pay. Institutions and government are still encouraged to give incentives and recognition.
★ Mutual or reciprocal benefits: both the volunteer and the people helped gain knowledge, skills, values, and networks.
⚙ Volunteerism also promotes respect and trust, inclusivity, and sustainability.
★ Remuneration: payment for work done.
★ Reciprocal: given and received by both sides.
EASIER: You choose to do it, you are not paid for it, and both sides gain from it.

WHY PEOPLE VOLUNTEER
★ To help others: to give back to society or make a difference in people's lives.
★ To belong to a group: volunteers want to feel valued.
★ To learn: to gain new skills, experience, or knowledge.

MODALITIES OF VOLUNTEERING
★ Modality: a way or form in which something is done.
⚙ People volunteer their time, talent, and treasure.
⚙ Skills, interests, and resources.
⚙ As an individual or as part of a group or organization.
⚙ Full-time or part-time; short-term or long-term.
⚙ Within or outside your organization or community.
⚙ In the country or overseas.

BENEFITS OF VOLUNTEERING
To the partner group or community:
⚙ Capacity building, partnership, and positive image.
⚙ For the community, the immediate effect is building organizational capacity. Volunteers help the community reach social services and economic opportunities aimed at sustainability and empowerment.
⚙ For the institution giving the volunteers, volunteering promotes its value and positive image. This brings support from key partners and makes it a model and leader in the community.
★ Capacity building: making a group stronger and more able to run and help itself.
To the society and government:
⚙ Economic contribution: volunteers are not paid, but their work, if counted in money, adds a lot to the country's economy.
⚙ Citizen participation in development: volunteering promotes participation and governance.
⚙ It builds social and human capital, the collective benefit of people working together, which is a very important asset of a nation.
★ Social capital: the value created when people interact, trust, and work together.
To the volunteer:
⚙ Knowledge, skills, and values; networks; self-fulfillment.
⚙ Community organizers develop communication, coordination, and leadership skills.
⚙ Project volunteers get first-hand experience in managerial work and build relationships with other groups.
⚙ These contacts and friendships can help future careers and employment.
⚙ The most important benefit for the volunteer is self-fulfillment: finding happiness and joy in helping.

VOLUNTEERING IN THE PHILIPPINES TODAY
⚙ Philippine volunteering is now a shared commitment among the different sectors of society.
★ Non-government (not-for-profit) organizations: provide complementary social and technical services in underserved communities.
★ Corporate sector: volunteering is part of the company's corporate social responsibility, through employee volunteering.
★ Academe: knowledge and skills sharing in the community, plus research and curriculum development.
★ Government: agencies are told to set up volunteer programs for the public and to encourage government employees to volunteer.
★ International volunteer service organizations: send foreign volunteers to give technical help where local expertise is not available, in line with national development priorities.
★ Corporate social responsibility (CSR): a company's duty to help society and the environment, not just make profit.
The cooperation of these sectors results in:
⚙ (a) Promotion of inclusivity in volunteering.
⚙ (b) Sharing of resources and information to strengthen each other's capacities.
⚙ (c) Partnerships for sustainability and lasting impact.
⚙ Emerging areas of volunteering: disaster response, corporate volunteerism, and youth volunteering.

COMMON MISTAKES
⚙ Saying volunteers must never receive anything. They do not expect pay, but incentives and recognition are encouraged.
⚙ Saying only the community benefits. The benefits are mutual: the volunteer gains too.
⚙ Mixing up the sectors: CSR is the corporate sector; research and curriculum is the academe.
⚙ Forgetting that the greatest benefit to the volunteer is self-fulfillment.

EXAM TIPS
⚙ Know the three elements: free will, no material reward, mutual benefits.
⚙ Know the three reasons people volunteer: to help others, to belong, to learn.
⚙ Know the benefits to the community, the society and government, and the volunteer.
⚙ Know the meaning of bayanihan and where the word comes from.
